Privacy Policy — Growminder

Effective date: 2026-09-11  ·  Developer: Collective Unconcious, Hong Kong, China

Growminder is a plant care tracking app. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is protected.

1. Information We Collect

Growminder does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers. The app runs entirely offline and does not request the INTERNET permission.

All content you enter — plant names, species / family / genus, locations, care logs (watering, fertilizing, pruning, repotting, pest control, measurement, photos), expense records, and memorial notes — is saved only in a private database on your own device.

2. Photos

Photos you add are stored locally on your device. They are never uploaded, shared, or sent to any third party.

3. Permissions Used

  • Notifications — to remind you of scheduled care tasks.
  • Alarms (exact alarm) — to trigger care reminders at the time you set.
  • NFC — to scan the QR tags you print for each plant.

None of these permissions are used to collect or transmit data.

4. Third-Party Services

Growminder uses no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs. No data leaves your device.

5. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Your Rights & Data Control

Because all data stays on your device, you fully control it. You can delete individual records inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove all local data. We cannot access or delete your data because we never receive it.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the effective date shown above.
